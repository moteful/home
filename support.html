Why it stays free

Every tool runs entirely in your browser. There are no servers to rent, no files to store and no cloud bills to pay — so keeping Moteful online costs very little.

Core tools stay free — and they will not shrink. Existing features are never moved behind a paywall, no ads are forced on anyone, and no sign-up is needed. Future advanced capabilities may be offered as an optional Pro tier. Tips are always optional.

Your support pays for new tools, fixes and translations. It never buys influence over what you can do — supporters use exactly the same tools as everyone else.
